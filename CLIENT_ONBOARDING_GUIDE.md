## 📋 The Five Stages Explained

### Stage 1: Discover (Week 1)
**What Happens:** We learn about your business, goals, and what you need.

**What We Do:**
- Have a kickoff meeting/call with you
- Ask questions about your business and goals
- Review competitor sites (for inspiration, not copying)
- Create a project brief document

**What You Do:**
- Attend the kickoff meeting/call
- Answer our questions honestly
- Share any existing logos, brand colors, or assets you have
- Review and approve the project brief within 5 business days

**Deliverable:** Project brief document you approve

**⏱️ Timeline:** Typically 1 week

---

### Stage 2: Design (Weeks 2-3)
**What Happens:** We create the visual design for your website.

**What We Do:**
- Design wireframes (blueprint of your site structure)
- Create beautiful design mockups for key pages
- Show you colors, fonts, and overall look
- Make revisions based on your feedback (number of rounds depends on your package)

**What You Do:**
- Review wireframes and designs
- Give us specific, clear feedback (e.g., "Make the headline bigger" not just "I don't like it")
- Approve the design before we start building

**Deliverables:** Wireframes, design mockups, design system

**⏱️ Timeline:** 1-2 weeks (depends on revision rounds)

**Revisions Included:**
- Essential: 2 rounds
- Professional: 3 rounds
- Complete: 4 rounds

**💡 Pro Tip:** Give all your feedback at once, not piece by piece. This keeps things moving faster!

---

### Stage 3: Build (Weeks 3-5)
**What Happens:** We turn the approved design into a real, working website.

**What We Do:**
- Build your website with clean, fast code
- Make sure it works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops
- Set up contact forms and any integrations you need
- Add basic SEO (so search engines can find you)
- Optimize for speed (target: under 2 seconds)

**What You Do:**
- **Provide all your content by Week 3** (text, images, logos)
- Review the site as we build it (we'll give you a staging link)
- Test features and tell us if anything's broken
- Answer questions quickly (within 3 business days)

**Deliverable:** Staging website (test version) you can review

**⏱️ Timeline:** 2-3 weeks (starts after design approval)

**⚠️ Important:** If you don't provide content by the deadline, your timeline will be extended. We can't build pages without content!

---

### Stage 4: Launch (Week 5-6)
**What Happens:** Final testing and going live with your website.

**What We Do:**
- Test everything one more time
- Make sure it works in all browsers
- Deploy your site to your hosting (we'll help you set this up)
- Give you all files and admin access
- Show you how to update content (if applicable)
- Answer any questions

**What You Do:**
- Do a final review of the staging site
- Give final approval (or request any last-minute fixes)
- Set up hosting (we can recommend providers)
- Attend the launch/training session (if applicable)

**Deliverables:** Live website, all files, admin access, documentation

**⏱️ Timeline:** 1 week

**🎉 Launch Day:** Once you approve and pay final invoice, we go live!

---

### Stage 5: Optimize (Post-Launch)
**What Happens:** We monitor and fix any issues after launch.

**What We Do:**
- Watch for bugs or broken features
- Fix any issues you report
- Make minor adjustments (within reason)
- Monitor performance

**What You Do:**
- Use your new website!
- Report any bugs or issues you find
- Keep content updated (if WordPress/Shopify)

**⏱️ Support Period:**
- Essential: 1 month post-launch
- Professional: 3 months post-launch
- Complete: 6 months post-launch

**Note:** Support covers bugs and minor fixes. New features or major changes cost extra.

## 📝 Your Responsibilities

### Content Provision
- **You provide:** All text, images, logos, and media
- **Deadline:** Typically Week 3 of project
- **Format:** We can work with most formats, but high-quality images are best
- **Legal:** Make sure you have rights to use all images/content

**Need help writing?** We can refer you to copywriters or provide content services (billed separately).

### Feedback
- **Be specific:** "Make the headline 20% bigger" is better than "I don't like it"
- **Be timely:** Respond within review periods to keep timeline on track
- **Be complete:** Give all feedback at once, not trickle it in

**Delayed feedback = delayed timeline** (but that's okay, we'll adjust)

### Access
- Provide hosting account access when needed (if you already have hosting)
- Provide domain access for DNS setup (if needed)
- We only request what we need, and you can change passwords after!

## 🚨 What Happens If...

### You want to change the scope?
1. Tell us what you want to add/change
2. We'll give you an estimate (time + cost)
3. You approve in writing
4. We adjust timeline and proceed

**No work without approval** - This protects both of us!

### You're not happy with something?
**Tell us immediately!** We want you to be happy. We can fix most things, but:
- Design changes after approval = extra cost
- Scope changes = extra cost
- But bugs and issues = free during support period

### The timeline needs to change?
**That's okay!** Just communicate with us. We'll adjust the timeline based on:
- Your availability
- Content delivery
- Scope changes

No penalties for delays (unless you need rush delivery originally agreed on).

### You want to cancel?
**You can cancel anytime** with 7 days written notice. You'll pay for work completed to date. Deposits aren't refundable after Discovery stage (because we've already done work).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### "What if I don't like the design?"
We include multiple revision rounds. Give us specific feedback, and we'll iterate until you're happy. If you need more rounds, we'll discuss additional costs.

### "Can I make changes after launch?"
Yes! During support period, minor changes are included. After that, changes are $150/hour. Or consider our maintenance plan.

### "What if I need it faster?"
Rush delivery (2-3 weeks) is available for +25% fee. You'll need to provide content and feedback within 24-48 hours.

### "Do I own my website?"
Yes! 100%. You own everything - code, content, design. You can take it anywhere, modify it, or switch developers anytime. No lock-ins.

### "What if something breaks after launch?"
Report it during your support period, and we'll fix it for free. After support period, we can fix it at our hourly rate or add you to maintenance.

### "Can I host anywhere I want?"
Yes! We don't lock you into any hosting provider. We'll help you set up wherever you choose.
