## 💡 Tips for Contract Management

### Before Sending:
- [ ] Review entire contract yourself
- [ ] Have lawyer review (highly recommended)
- [ ] Customize governing law section
- [ ] Update business information
- [ ] Fill in package details for each client
- [ ] Save as PDF for professional presentation

### When Presenting to Clients:
- ✅ Be transparent - explain that this protects both parties
- ✅ Highlight that they own everything (no lock-ins)
- ✅ Point out the revision limits (sets expectations)
- ✅ Show them the onboarding guide so they know what to expect
- ✅ Encourage questions before signing

### Red Flags to Watch For:
- ⚠️ Client wants to remove liability limitations (red flag)
- ⚠️ Client wants unlimited revisions (adjust scope/price)
- ⚠️ Client wants guarantees on outcomes (avoid)
- ⚠️ Client wants ownership of your code libraries (unreasonable)
- ⚠️ Client wants to pay after completion (risky)

## 🚨 Common Scenarios Handled

The contract protects you in these situations:

### Scenario 1: Client wants unlimited revisions
**Contract Protection:** Limits revisions to 2-4 rounds. Additional rounds at $150/hour.

### Scenario 2: Client doesn't provide content on time
**Contract Protection:** Timeline extends automatically. No penalty to you.

### Scenario 3: Client wants major scope change
**Contract Protection:** Requires written estimate and approval before proceeding.

### Scenario 4: Client doesn't pay final invoice
**Contract Protection:** No file transfer until payment. Late fees apply.

### Scenario 5: Client modifies site and breaks it
**Contract Protection:** Not covered during support period if client caused it.

### Scenario 6: Client expects guaranteed results (traffic, sales)
**Contract Protection:** No warranties clause - best effort basis only.

### Scenario 7: Client wants refund after completion
**Contract Protection:** No refunds after completion clearly stated.

### Scenario 8: Client wants to cancel mid-project
**Contract Protection:** Pays for work completed. Deposit non-refundable after Discovery.
