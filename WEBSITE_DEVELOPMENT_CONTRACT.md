## 2. PROJECT STAGES AND PROCESS

This section outlines the five-stage process we follow for every website project. Each stage includes specific deliverables, review periods, and approval requirements.

### STAGE 1: DISCOVER (Week 1)

**Purpose:** Understand your business, goals, target audience, and requirements.

**Our Responsibilities:**
- Conduct discovery meeting/call
- Review your business objectives and goals
- Analyze target audience and market positioning
- Review competitor sites (for inspiration only, not copying)
- Document technical requirements
- Create project brief and strategy document

**Your Responsibilities:**
- Attend discovery meeting/call
- Provide business information and goals
- Share any existing branding guidelines or assets
- Respond to discovery questions within 3 business days

**Deliverables:**
- Project brief document
- Technical requirements specification
- Content requirements checklist

**Review Period:** 5 business days for client review and approval of project brief

**Milestone:** Project brief approved → Proceed to Design stage

---

### STAGE 2: DESIGN (Week 2-3)

**Purpose:** Create wireframes, mockups, and design prototypes for your website.

**Our Responsibilities:**
- Create site structure/wireframes
- Design homepage and key pages (based on package)
- Create design system (colors, typography, components)
- Provide design mockups for review
- Implement feedback and revisions (see Revision Policy, Section 4)

**Your Responsibilities:**
- Review wireframes within 5 business days
- Provide specific, actionable feedback
- Approve design direction before build begins
- Provide brand assets (logos, images) if available

**Deliverables:**
- Site structure/wireframes
- Design mockups (3-5 pages based on package)
- Design system documentation

**Review Period:** 5 business days for each design round

**Number of Design Rounds Included:**
- Essential Package: 2 rounds of revisions
- Professional Package: 3 rounds of revisions
- Complete Package: 4 rounds of revisions

**Additional Design Revisions:** $150/hour (see Revision Policy)

**Milestone:** Design approved → Proceed to Build stage

---

### STAGE 3: BUILD (Week 3-5)

**Purpose:** Develop the website with clean code, responsive layouts, and performance optimization.

**Our Responsibilities:**
- Build website according to approved designs
- Ensure mobile responsiveness (all devices)
- Implement basic SEO setup (meta tags, sitemaps, technical SEO)
- Set up contact forms and integrations (if included)
- Configure analytics (if included in package)
- Perform quality assurance testing
- Optimize for performance (target: under 2 seconds load time)

**Your Responsibilities:**
- Provide all text content, images, and media by [DATE]
- Review development updates in staging environment
- Test functionality and provide feedback
- Respond to questions within 3 business days

**Deliverables:**
- Fully functional staging website
- Basic SEO implementation
- Mobile-responsive design
- Contact forms and integrations (as specified)
- Performance optimization

**Review Period:** Ongoing during build, with staging access provided

**Content Deadline:** Content must be provided by Week 3 of project start. Delays will extend timeline.

**Milestone:** Build complete → Proceed to Launch stage

---

### STAGE 4: LAUNCH (Week 5-6)

**Purpose:** Final testing, deployment, and handoff of your website.

**Our Responsibilities:**
- Final quality assurance testing
- Cross-browser compatibility testing
- Accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA standards)
- Performance testing and optimization
- Deploy website to production (client's hosting)
- Provide training on content management (if applicable)
- Deliver all files and documentation
- Transfer admin access and ownership

**Your Responsibilities:**
- Final review and approval of staging site
- Provide hosting account access (or purchase hosting)
- Provide domain access (if needed for DNS configuration)
- Attend launch/training session (if applicable)
- Sign off on final deliverables

**Deliverables:**
- Live production website
- All source code files
- Admin access credentials
- Documentation and training materials
- Content management system access (if applicable)

**Review Period:** 5 business days for final approval

**Launch Support Included:**
- Essential Package: Launch assistance + 1 month support
- Professional Package: Launch assistance + 3 months support
- Complete Package: Launch assistance + 6 months support

**Milestone:** Website launched and approved → Project completion

---

### STAGE 5: OPTIMIZE (Post-Launch)

**Purpose:** Post-launch monitoring, tweaks, and improvements.

**Our Responsibilities:**
- Monitor website performance for [support period]
- Address any bugs or issues (see Support Period, Section 5)
- Provide minor adjustments within scope
- Performance monitoring and recommendations

**Your Responsibilities:**
- Report any bugs or issues promptly
- Provide feedback on website performance
- Keep content and plugins updated (if WordPress/Shopify)

**Deliverables:**
- Bug fixes and minor adjustments
- Performance reports (if applicable)
- Recommendations for improvements

**Support Period:**
- Essential Package: 1 month post-launch support
- Professional Package: 3 months post-launch support
- Complete Package: 6 months post-launch support

**Note:** Post-launch support covers bug fixes and minor adjustments only. New features, major changes, or additional pages are outside this scope and will be billed separately.
