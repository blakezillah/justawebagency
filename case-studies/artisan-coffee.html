Category Web + Brand

The Challenge

Artisan Coffee Co. had an outdated website that didn't reflect their premium brand or support their growing e-commerce needs. Their old site was slow, difficult to manage, and didn't convert visitors into customers. They needed a complete rebrand and a modern e-commerce platform that could scale with their business.

Our Approach

Brand Identity

We developed a warm, inviting brand identity that reflected their artisanal approach to coffee. The new logo, color palette, and typography system created a cohesive brand experience across all touchpoints.

E-commerce Strategy

We built a custom Shopify store with a focus on product discovery and conversion. The site features intuitive navigation, beautiful product photography, and a streamlined checkout process.

Performance Optimization

Every element was optimized for speed. We implemented lazy loading, optimized images, and minimized JavaScript to achieve a 2.1-second load time—critical for e-commerce conversion rates.
