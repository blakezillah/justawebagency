Local Restaurant

CategoryWeb
PlatformWordPress
Timeline5 weeks
FocusMobile-first

The Challenge

A local restaurant needed a website that would showcase their menu, enable online ordering, and be easy to update. Their previous site was difficult to maintain and didn't work well on mobile devices. They needed a mobile-first solution that would help them attract customers and take orders online.

Our Approach

Menu-Focused Design

We designed the site with the menu as the hero element. Beautiful food photography and easy-to-read menu items make it simple for customers to browse and order.

Online Ordering Integration

We integrated a seamless online ordering system that allows customers to place orders directly from the website, with real-time order tracking.

Easy Content Management

Built on WordPress with a custom theme, the restaurant can easily update menus, add specials, and manage content without technical knowledge.
