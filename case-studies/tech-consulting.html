The Challenge

A tech consulting firm needed a professional website that would generate qualified leads and establish thought leadership in their industry. Their previous site was outdated and didn't reflect their expertise. They needed a content-driven site with a blog, lead capture forms, and strong SEO foundations.

Our Approach

Content Strategy

We developed a content strategy focused on addressing their target audience's pain points. The blog became a lead generation engine, with each post designed to capture leads through valuable resources and consultation offers.

Lead Generation System

We built a comprehensive lead capture system with multiple touchpoints: homepage CTA, blog post CTAs, resource downloads, and consultation booking forms. Every element was optimized for conversion.

SEO Foundations

Strong on-page SEO, technical optimization, and content structure resulted in 3x organic traffic growth. The site ranks for high-value keywords in their industry.
